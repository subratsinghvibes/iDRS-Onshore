How iDRS Works

From data input to optimized schedule in three simple steps

Upload Your Data

Import rig specifications and well requirements via CSV files or manual entry. Include GPS coordinates, technical specs, availability windows, and cost parameters.

Mathematical Optimization

Click optimize and watch Google OR-Tools analyze thousands of possible combinations, considering all constraints and objectives to find the mathematically optimal schedule.

Interactive Refinement

Use the drag-and-drop Gantt chart to make manual adjustments. The system automatically re-optimizes the entire schedule, maintaining constraint compliance.
