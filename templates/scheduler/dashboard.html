Welcome to the Interactive Drilling Rig Scheduler

The Interactive Drilling Rig Scheduler (IDRS) represents a revolutionary approach to drilling operations management, utilizing advanced optimization algorithms to efficiently assign drilling rigs to wells while minimizing operational costs and maximizing resource utilization.

Our sophisticated system evaluates thousands of possible rig-well combinations in real-time, incorporating critical factors such as rig capacity specifications, well complexity parameters, inter-location travel distances, operational priorities, and temporal constraints. The platform delivers comprehensive schedule visualization through interactive Gantt charts and detailed cost analysis reports.

Beyond scheduling, the platform delivers intelligent insights that adapt to changing field conditions, combining intuitive visuals with analytical modeling to bridge strategy and execution, enabling data-driven, responsive decisions across all stakeholder levels.

Optimizer Constraints

The scheduling optimizer enforces the following constraints:

  • Assignment: Each well is assigned to at most one rig
  • No Overlap: A rig cannot drill multiple wells simultaneously
  • Rig Availability: Wells can only be drilled during rig's contract period (start/end dates)
  • Ready-to-Drill (RTD): Wells cannot start before their RTD date
  • Rig Capacity: Rig horsepower must meet or exceed well's HP requirement
  • Drilling Depth: Rig's drilling capacity must accommodate well depth
  • BOP Stack: Rig's BOP stack rating must meet well requirements
  • TDS Equipment: If well requires TDS, rig must have TDS availability

The optimizer minimizes total project duration + inter-location movement costs while minimizing the number of unassigned wells (especially HIGH priority).
