Understanding Data Management
Wells Tab

Wells have 2 stages:

  • Staged Wells: Wells imported from SAP's loc_release_data. These are staged until complete information is either manually entered or derived from IDT norms.
  • Final Wells: Shown in the Manage Wells table. Stats cards provide at-a-glance information about your wells.

Baskets Feature: Process a few staged wells at a time – useful for prioritizing handful of wells needed earlier or to sense-check a schedule before processing all.

Manage Wells: Add, delete, upload, edit, or download wells directly from this section.

Rigs Tab

Rig Mapping is essential to associate rigs with Rig Building Norms and derive ILM (Inter-Location Movement) days.

Add, delete, upload, edit, or download rigs directly from Manage Rigs section.

IDT Norms Tab

IDT records all norms as issued by the IDT department and maps them here as tables: Benchmarks, Daily Drilling Rate, Rig Building, Add. Ops Drilling, Completion Test, and Additional Tests.

Special Feature – Location-Field Combinations: Maintain data integrity across concerned tables. Edit a field name once and it updates across all instances. This helps easily manage and work with well data completion, ensuring uniformity.

ILM Distances Tab

Once Rigs, Wells, and IDT Norms data is complete, run Rig-Well combinations to generate ILM costs. These costs are used in the Scheduler for more accurate scheduling.
