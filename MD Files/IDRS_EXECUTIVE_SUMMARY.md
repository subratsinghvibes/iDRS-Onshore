# Interactive Drilling Rig Scheduler (IDRS)
## Executive Summary

**Version:** 9.0 | **Date:** February 2026

## What is IDRS?

The **Interactive Drilling Rig Scheduler (IDRS)** is an advanced, AI-powered platform that revolutionizes drilling operations planning and management. It combines intelligent optimization, comprehensive data management, and powerful analytics to deliver optimal drilling schedules in minutes instead of weeks.

---

## Three-Pillar Architecture

### 🗄️ Pillar 1: Data Management
**Foundation for Excellence**

- Centralized repository for rigs, wells, and operational data
- Intelligent data enrichment and validation
- Bulk import/export capabilities
- Well basket management with smart suggestions
- Historical benchmarks and performance norms

**Key Features:**
- 500+ wells and 50+ rigs capacity
- Automated data validation
- Intelligent data fetching
- Location-based organization
- Comprehensive audit trails

---

### ⚙️ Pillar 2: Scheduling & Optimization
**Core Intelligence Engine**

- Google OR-Tools powered optimization
- Minimizes Inter-Location Movement (ILM) costs
- Respects all operational constraints
- Generates optimal schedules in 30-300 seconds
- Handles complex multi-rig, multi-well scenarios

**Key Capabilities:**
- Automatic rig-well matching
- Priority-based scheduling
- Constraint satisfaction
- Scenario analysis
- Schedule branching

---

### 📊 Pillar 3: Post-Scheduling Analysis
**Insights & Intelligence**

- Interactive Gantt charts
- Geographic movement maps
- Performance dashboards
- Comparative analysis
- Comprehensive reporting

**Key Tools:**
- Real-time visualization
- Cost analysis
- Utilization tracking
- Export capabilities
- Stakeholder reporting
